About The Project

Where medicine, law, ethics, and technology meet.

Who Created It?

BioEthix & MedLaw Assistant was built as an open-source educational project by a student developer exploring the intersection of modern healthcare, AI, and legal ethics.

Why Does It Exist?

As AI and automated systems rapidly enter healthcare, complex questions arise regarding liability, patient autonomy, consent, and safety. This tool provides an accessible platform to break down these dilemmas systematically.

Who Is It For?

  • Students: Exploring medical law and bioethics topics.
  • Educators & Debaters: Sourcing structured case studies for discussion.
  • Researchers: Analyzing interdisciplinary healthcare policy questions.

Academic & Educational Disclaimer

This platform is designed purely for educational exploration, policy analysis, and classroom debate. It does not provide professional medical or legal advice. Users should consult qualified legal counsel or healthcare professionals for official guidance.
